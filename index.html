About IMOLE

A New Voice. A Better Ojo.

A grassroots movement focused on practical representation, community development and accountable leadership.

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a grassroots leader and community-focused candidate for Lagos State House of Assembly, Ojo Constituency II.

His vision is to promote better infrastructure, quality education, accessible healthcare, youth and women empowerment, community development and transparent representation.

🤝 Accessibility A leader who listens to the people.
🛡️ Integrity Leadership built on honesty.
📊 Accountability Responsible representation.
❤️ Compassion People first, always.

Our Priorities

Service, Development & Accountability

The IMOLE 2027 movement is built around practical issues affecting everyday life in Ojo Constituency II.

🎓

Quality Education

Support better schools, learning opportunities, skills acquisition and educational development.

🏥

Better Healthcare

Advocate for accessible and quality healthcare services across the constituency.

🛣️

Roads & Drainage

Promote better roads, drainage and essential public infrastructure.

💼

Youth Empowerment

Promote skills, employment opportunities, entrepreneurship and economic participation.

👩‍👧

Women Development

Support opportunities and initiatives that empower women across Ojo Constituency II.

🤝

Community Development

Strengthen grassroots participation and community-focused development.
