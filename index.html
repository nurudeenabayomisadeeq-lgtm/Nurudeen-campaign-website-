MEET THE CANDIDATE

A Son Of Ojo, For The People Of Ojo

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II who understands the people, the communities and the challenges facing residents.

His campaign is built around practical leadership, accessibility, compassion, accountability and meaningful development.

Integrity Leadership people can trust.
Accessibility A representative who listens.
Accountability Transparent service to the people.
Compassion People-centred leadership.
