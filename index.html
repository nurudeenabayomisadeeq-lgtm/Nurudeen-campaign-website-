Candidate

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is the IMOLE 2027 candidate for the Lagos State House of Assembly, Ojo Constituency II.

The campaign is built around the principles of Service, Development and Accountability, with a focus on accessible representation and community development.

Integrity Leadership built on honesty and trust.
Accessibility A representative who remains close to the people.
Humility Respect for every member of the constituency.
Accountability Responsible service and transparent leadership.
