About Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

A New Voice for Ojo Constituency II

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a dedicated community leader and Lagos State House of Assembly candidate under the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) for Ojo Constituency II.

He is committed to quality education, accessible healthcare, better roads and drainage, youth and women empowerment, security, transparency, and accountable leadership.

His vision is to build a better Ojo Constituency II where every resident has equal opportunities to succeed and where government truly serves the people.

About Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a dedicated grassroots leader, community developer and passionate advocate for good governance. He is the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) candidate for the Lagos State House of Assembly, Ojo Constituency II.

For many years, he has remained committed to serving the people through community engagement, youth empowerment, educational support and humanitarian activities. His leadership is built on integrity, accountability, accessibility and selfless service.
