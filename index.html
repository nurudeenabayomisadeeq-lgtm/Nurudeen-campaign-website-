Meet the Candidate

Leadership That Starts With Listening

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is presenting his vision for Ojo Constituency II with an emphasis on community development, accessibility and accountability.

From roads and drainage to education, healthcare and support for local traders, the needs of our communities deserve attention, responsible representation and practical follow-through.

IMOLE 2027 is built around the belief that public service should remain connected to the everyday experiences and aspirations of the people it serves.

Integrity Responsible and principled service
Accessibility Listening and remaining approachable
Humility Respect for every community
Accountability Transparency in public service

Our Vision for Ojo

Development That Matters

A people-centred vision focused on the everyday needs, opportunities and long-term development of Ojo Constituency II.

🛣️

Roads and Drainage

Advocate for attention to road infrastructure, drainage challenges and the flooding concerns affecting homes, markets and communities.

🏫

Education and Young People

Promote educational opportunities, skills development and initiatives that help young people prepare for a productive future.

🏥

Healthcare

Support advocacy for accessible healthcare facilities and improved attention to the health needs of local communities.

🛍️

Traders and Small Businesses

Champion engagement with traders and small businesses, with attention to the challenges affecting local economic activity.

🤝

Community Engagement

Create opportunities for residents, community associations and local stakeholders to communicate their needs and priorities.

📋

Accountability

Encourage transparent communication, responsible representation and continued engagement with the people of the constituency.
