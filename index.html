WHO IS NURUDEEN?

Leadership That Lives Among The People

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II who understands the people, communities and everyday challenges of the constituency.

The IMOLE 2027 vision is built on a simple principle: public office must be about service to the people and measurable development.
