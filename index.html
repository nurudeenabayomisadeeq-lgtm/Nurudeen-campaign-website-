A Son of Ojo. A Voice for the People.

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II who understands the hopes, challenges and aspirations of the people.

Having grown up and worked within the community, he believes leadership must be accessible, compassionate, responsible and accountable.

As a businessman and community-minded leader, his vision is to promote practical representation that puts residents first.

OUR COMMITMENT

Our Manifesto for Ojo Constituency II

Practical priorities for a stronger, safer and more prosperous constituency.

01

Road Development

Advocate for better roads, access routes and community infrastructure across Ojo Constituency II.

02

Drainage and Flood Control

Push for improved drainage systems and sustainable solutions to recurring flooding.

03

Education

Support schools, scholarships, learning resources and educational opportunities for our children.

04

Healthcare

Advocate for functional health centres, improved medical facilities and accessible healthcare.

05

Youth Empowerment

Promote skills acquisition, entrepreneurship, mentorship and employment opportunities.

06

Market Development

Champion better facilities, sanitation, security and support for traders and artisans.

07

Riverine Communities

Advocate for clean water, bridges, transport access and improved public services.

08

Women Development

Support women's participation, enterprise, leadership and economic opportunities.

09

Community Security

Encourage collaboration between communities and relevant security institutions.

10

Accountable Representation

Promote regular town halls, transparent communication and accessible representation.

11

Environmental Sanitation

Advocate for cleaner streets, proper waste management and a healthier environment.

12

Inclusive Development

Ensure that every ward and community has a voice in constituency development.
