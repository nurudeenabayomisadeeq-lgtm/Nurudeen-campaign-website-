Community priorities

Listening to the issues that shape daily life

Residents can use the website to share concerns and ideas. The following areas are priorities for community discussion, not claims that projects have already been delivered.

⌁

Roads and drainage

Document road access problems, flooding and blocked drains.

✚

Health and sanitation

Highlight concerns about health facilities, water and sanitation.

▤

Schools and skills

Raise education infrastructure and youth development concerns.

⌂

Markets and livelihoods

Share traders' concerns about market facilities and livelihoods.

✦

Youth and women

Suggest opportunities, training and inclusive community programmes.

◎

Accountability

Encourage transparent follow-up and clear community reporting.
