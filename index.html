OUR COMMITMENT

Our Manifesto for Ojo Constituency II

Practical priorities for a stronger, safer and more prosperous constituency.

01

Road Development

Advocate for better roads, access routes and community infrastructure across Ojo Constituency II.

02

Drainage and Flood Control

Push for improved drainage systems and sustainable solutions to recurring flooding.

03

Education

Support schools, scholarships, learning resources and educational opportunities for our children.

04

Healthcare

Advocate for functional health centres, improved medical facilities and accessible healthcare.

05

Youth Empowerment

Promote skills acquisition, entrepreneurship, mentorship and employment opportunities.

06

Market Development

Champion better facilities, sanitation, security and support for traders and artisans.

07

Riverine Communities

Advocate for clean water, bridges, transport access and improved public services.

08

Women Development

Support women's participation, enterprise, leadership and economic opportunities.

09

Community Security

Encourage collaboration between communities and relevant security institutions.

10

Accountable Representation

Promote regular town halls, transparent communication and accessible representation.

11

Environmental Sanitation

Advocate for cleaner streets, proper waste management and a healthier environment.

12

Inclusive Development

Ensure that every ward and community has a voice in constituency development.

MEET OUR LEADERS AND CANDIDATES

Seven Profiles. One Shared Vision.

Meet the political leaders and candidates featured in the IMOLE 2027 campaign platform.

NATIONAL POLITICAL LEADER

Mr. Peter Obi

Mr. Peter Obi is a prominent Nigerian political figure whose public message has centred on prudent leadership, accountability, productivity and people-focused governance.

Public focus: Responsible leadership • Economic opportunity • Accountability

NATIONAL POLITICAL LEADER

Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso

Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso is a recognised Nigerian political leader associated with public administration, political organisation and advocacy for education and human-capital development.

Public focus: Education • Human-capital development • Public leadership

NDC LEADERSHIP

Hon. Moshood Salvador

Hon. Moshood Salvador is part of the wider NDC political leadership and party engagement structure, contributing to political organisation and stakeholder participation.

Leadership focus: Party organisation • Stakeholder engagement • Political participation

NDC LEADERSHIP

Funsho Doherty

Funsho Doherty is a public figure and political leader whose public engagement has emphasised transparency, responsible governance and stronger public institutions.

Public focus: Transparency • Good governance • Public accountability

OJO FEDERAL CONSTITUENCY

Hon. Seyi Sowunmi

Hon. Seyi Sowunmi is the Ojo Federal Constituency candidate featured in this campaign platform. His campaign vision centres on effective federal representation, listening to constituents and bringing Ojo's development needs to the national level.

Constituency focus: Federal representation • Community development • Accountability

OJO CONSTITUENCY I

Hon. Adisa Adeosun

Hon. Adisa Adeosun is presented as the NDC candidate for Ojo Constituency I, with a focus on responsive state-level representation and ensuring that community priorities are heard.

Constituency focus: Constituency representation • Public services • Community voice

OJO CONSTITUENCY II • IMOLE 2027

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II who grew up and has worked within the community. As a businessman and community-minded leader, he believes representation must be accessible, compassionate and accountable.

His vision is to advocate for practical improvements in roads, drainage, education, healthcare, market facilities, youth and women's opportunities, while ensuring that every ward has a voice.

Constituency focus: Roads and drainage • Education and healthcare • Youth and women empowerment • Accountable representation
