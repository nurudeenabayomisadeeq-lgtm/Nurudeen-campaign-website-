About IMOLE 2027

A vision for accessible leadership, community development and accountability in Ojo Constituency II.

Our Mission

To promote responsive representation that listens to residents, recognises local priorities and works responsibly towards practical community development.

Our Values

Integrity, accessibility, humility, accountability, compassion and respect for the people are important values guiding the IMOLE 2027 vision.

Our Constituency

Ojo Constituency II is home to diverse communities, families, traders, artisans, young people, workers and community leaders whose voices matter.

Our Commitment

To listen to community concerns and promote constructive conversations about roads, drainage, education, healthcare, local enterprise and public accountability.

Service. Development. Accountability.

Our priorities begin with listening to the people and understanding the issues affecting everyday life.

🛣️

Roads & Infrastructure

Promote attention to road conditions, drainage challenges and other infrastructure concerns affecting residents and businesses.

🏥

Healthcare

Support constructive engagement around accessible, dependable healthcare services and the needs of local health centres.

📚

Education & Young People

Promote educational opportunities, skills development and initiatives that help young people prepare for the future.

🛍️

Traders & Small Businesses

Listen to market traders, artisans and small-business owners about the challenges that affect their livelihoods.

🤝

Community Engagement

Encourage regular dialogue with residents, community associations, traditional institutions and other local stakeholders.

📋

Accountability

Encourage open communication, responsible public service and clear explanations of priorities, progress and challenges.
