Meet the Candidate

Leadership With Purpose

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is the NDC candidate for the Lagos State House of Assembly, Ojo Constituency II.

As a son of Ojo Constituency II, his campaign is focused on listening to residents, strengthening communities and promoting practical development that touches everyday life.

IMOLE represents a commitment to responsible representation, accessibility, accountability and meaningful service to the people.

Integrity Leadership people can trust.
Accessibility A representative who listens.
Accountability Responsible stewardship.
Compassion People-centred representation.
Our Vision

Service • Development • Accountability

A practical agenda focused on the needs and aspirations of Ojo Constituency II.

🏗️

Roads & Drainage

Advocate for improved roads, drainage systems and infrastructure that make communities safer and easier to access.

🏥

Healthcare

Support stronger primary healthcare facilities and improved access to essential health services.

🎓

Education

Promote better learning environments, opportunities for young people and educational development.

🛍️

Traders & Businesses

Champion the interests of traders, artisans and local businesses while supporting economic opportunities.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Community Development

Encourage inclusive development that reaches residents across Ojo Constituency II.

🤝

People First

Build a culture of listening, transparency and accountability between representatives and constituents.
