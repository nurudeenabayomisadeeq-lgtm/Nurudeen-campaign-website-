Meet the Candidate

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

A son of Ojo Constituency II, Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq understands the importance of listening to residents and working with communities to address their needs.

His vision places emphasis on accessible representation, improved infrastructure, stronger community participation, opportunities for young people and accountability in public service.

IMOLE 2027 represents a commitment to constructive engagement and a better future for Ijanikin, Ilogbo, Irewe, Tafi, Etegbin and Idolowu.
