ABOUT IMOLE 2027

Leadership Built Around Service

A people-centred vision for responsible, accessible and accountable representation.

IMOLE 2027

Service. Development. Accountability.

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is presented as a community-focused candidate committed to listening to residents and supporting practical development across Ojo Constituency II.

The IMOLE 2027 vision places emphasis on accessibility, responsible representation, community development and transparent communication with the people.

🤝 Accessibility Leadership that listens and remains connected to the people.
🛡️ Integrity Responsible and transparent public service.
🏗️ Development Practical attention to community needs and opportunities.
❤️ Compassion People-centred representation and community engagement.
