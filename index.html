About IMOLE

Leadership With Purpose

A campaign focused on listening, serving and delivering meaningful development to the people.

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is presented as a son of Ojo Constituency II who understands the aspirations, challenges and opportunities within the constituency.

The IMOLE 2027 campaign is built around a simple commitment: Service, Development and Accountability.
