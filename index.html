ABOUT THE MOVEMENT

Leadership Built Around Service

Service, Development & Accountability

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is presented as a community-focused candidate committed to listening to residents and supporting practical development across Ojo Constituency II.

The IMOLE 2027 platform focuses on accessibility, responsible representation, community development and accountability.

🤝 Accessibility Leadership that listens and remains available.
🛡️ Integrity Responsible and transparent public service.
🏗️ Development Focus on practical community needs.
❤️ Compassion People-centred representation.
