About the Candidate

A Voice for Ojo Constituency II

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II, with a strong connection to the people and communities of the constituency.

The IMOLE 2027 vision is centred on accessible representation, responsible leadership, community development and accountability.
