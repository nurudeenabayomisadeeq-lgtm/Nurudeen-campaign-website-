Our Agenda

Service • Development • Accountability

The IMOLE vision is centred on practical representation, responsive leadership and community-focused development.

01

Better Infrastructure

Focus attention on roads, drainage and other community infrastructure priorities.

02

Healthcare

Champion accessible and responsive healthcare for residents.

03

Education

Support better educational opportunities and youth development.

04

Traders & Small Businesses

Champion the interests and economic needs of traders and local entrepreneurs.

05

Youth Empowerment

Encourage skills, opportunity, participation and responsible leadership among young people.

06

Accountability

Promote accessible representation and transparent communication with constituents.
