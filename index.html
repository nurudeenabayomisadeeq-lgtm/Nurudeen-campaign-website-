Meet Your Candidate

A VOICE FOR THE PEOPLE

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is presented as a candidate for the Lagos State House of Assembly representing Ojo Constituency II under the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

IMOLE 2027 is built around a commitment to service, development, accountability and meaningful engagement with residents and community stakeholders.
