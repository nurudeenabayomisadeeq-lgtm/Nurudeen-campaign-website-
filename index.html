Know the person

A community-first approach to representation.

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq's public profile is rooted in Ojo Constituency II and the everyday needs of its residents. His campaign message emphasises listening, accessibility, integrity and responsible representation.

Integrity Accessibility Humility Accountability Compassion

Our commitment

Listen carefully. Represent responsibly. Communicate clearly. Follow up on community concerns and report progress honestly. Development requires partnership between residents, communities and public institutions.
