About IMOLE 2027

Leadership that listens. Representation that delivers.

A Son Of Ojo, A Voice For The People

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is an indigene and son of Ojo Constituency II who understands the communities, the people and the challenges facing the constituency.

The IMOLE 2027 vision is built around service, development, accountability and accessible representation.

The movement seeks to give the people a stronger voice and a representative who remains close to the people.
