ABOUT THE CANDIDATE

Leadership rooted in the community

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II, committed to listening to residents and promoting practical solutions to the needs of our communities.

His vision is a constituency where public representation means accessibility, responsibility and measurable development.

OUR COMMITMENT

Service, Development and Accountability

A practical development agenda shaped by the needs and aspirations of Ojo Constituency II.

01

Roads and Drainage

Advocate improved roads, effective drainage and sustainable solutions to recurring flooding in our communities.

02

Education

Promote better learning environments, support for schools and opportunities for young people.

03

Healthcare

Support accessible primary healthcare, improved health facilities and community health awareness.

04

Jobs and Skills

Promote vocational training, entrepreneurship and opportunities for young people and artisans.

05

Markets and Traders

Advocate better market infrastructure, sanitation and support for small businesses and traders.

06

Accountable Representation

Maintain open communication with residents and provide regular updates on constituency matters.
