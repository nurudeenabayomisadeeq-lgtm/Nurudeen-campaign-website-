About IMOLE 2027

Service Before Self

A grassroots movement focused on responsible, accessible and accountable representation.

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is presented by the campaign as a son of Ojo Constituency II who grew up within the community and understands the needs, aspirations and challenges of the people.

  • Integrity
  • Accessibility
  • Humility
  • Accountability
  • Compassion

Our Vision

To promote practical community development, responsive representation and stronger opportunities for residents of Ojo Constituency II.

  • Better roads and drainage
  • Improved health facilities
  • Better educational opportunities
  • Support for traders and small businesses
  • Youth and women empowerment
