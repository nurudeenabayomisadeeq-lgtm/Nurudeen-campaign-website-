THE MOVEMENT

A New Light for Ojo Constituency II

IMOLE 2027 represents a commitment to people-centred representation and practical community development.

The IMOLE Vision

To build an accessible, responsible and development-focused representation where the needs of ordinary people remain at the centre of government.


From our communities to the legislative chamber, the mission is simple: listen, serve, act and remain accountable.

About Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II who understands the communities, the people and the everyday challenges confronting residents.

IMOLE 2027 is built around a practical approach to representation — one that prioritises accessibility, development, accountability and compassion.
