WHO IS NURUDEEN?

Leadership That Lives Among The People

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II who understands the people, communities and everyday challenges of the constituency.

The IMOLE 2027 vision is built on a simple principle: public office must be about service to the people and measurable development.

✓ Integrity Doing what is right.
✓ Accessibility Leadership within reach.
✓ Humility Serving with respect.
✓ Accountability Answerable to the people.
✓ Compassion Putting people first.
✓ Service Results over promises.

OUR VISION

Service. Development. Accountability.

A practical agenda focused on the real needs of the people of Ojo Constituency II.

🛣️

Roads & Drainage

Champion practical interventions for roads, drainage and infrastructure that improve movement, safety and local commerce.

🏥

Healthcare

Advocate for stronger health centres, improved access to healthcare and support for vulnerable residents.

🎓

Education

Support quality education, youth development and opportunities that help young people build productive futures.

🛍️

Traders & Businesses

Champion initiatives that recognise traders, artisans, entrepreneurs and small businesses.

👩

Women Empowerment

Promote stronger opportunities for women through inclusion, representation and community-focused initiatives.

👨‍💻

Youth Development

Give young people a stronger voice and promote skills, entrepreneurship, participation and opportunity.
