Our Manifesto Priorities

Practical priorities focused on improving everyday life across Ojo Constituency II.

1

Roads & Drainage

Advocate for better roads, drainage and infrastructure across our communities.

2

Healthcare

Champion improved access to functional healthcare facilities and community health services.

3

Education

Support initiatives that strengthen schools, learning environments and educational opportunities.

4

Traders & Businesses

Champion the interests of traders, artisans, entrepreneurs and local businesses.

5

Youth Development

Promote opportunities for young people through skills, participation and community development.

6

Accountable Representation

Promote accessibility, transparency and accountability in public representation.
