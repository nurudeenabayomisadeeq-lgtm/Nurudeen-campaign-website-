What Can You Report?

You can report concerns relating to:

  • Bad roads and transport
  • Flooding and blocked drainage
  • Schools and learning facilities
  • Health centres
  • Markets and local businesses
  • Water, sanitation and waste
  • Electricity and street lighting
  • Youth skills and development
  • Other community concerns

Important Information

This form is for community feedback, not emergency reporting. For an immediate danger or emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service.

Do not include passwords, bank details, national identification numbers or another person's private information.
