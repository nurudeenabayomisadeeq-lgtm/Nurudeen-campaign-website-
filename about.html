About the Candidate

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a candidate for the Lagos State House of Assembly representing Ojo Constituency II under the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

His campaign is centred on meaningful representation, community engagement and responsible public service.

The IMOLE 2027 movement seeks to bring together residents, volunteers, young people, women, community stakeholders and supporters who believe in constructive participation in the development of Ojo Constituency II.
