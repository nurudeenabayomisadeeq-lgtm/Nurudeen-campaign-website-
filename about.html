The Man Behind the Service

A Record of Public Service

From parliamentary representation to constituency initiatives, his public work has increasingly focused on practical opportunities for people and communities.

Hon. Oluwaseyi Ayopo Sowunmi was born on 12 April 1973 and holds a B.Sc. qualification. He represents Ojo Federal Constituency of Lagos State in the House of Representatives.

He was elected to the House of Representatives in the 2023 general election on the platform of the Labour Party (LP). Following a legal challenge to the election, the National Assembly Elections Petition Tribunal in Lagos initially nullified the result in August 2023. The Court of Appeal subsequently set aside the tribunal's decision and affirmed his election.

Within the National Assembly, Hon. Sowunmi has served as Deputy Chairman of the House Committee on Local Content. His committee responsibilities have also included areas such as Science and Technology, Foreign Aid, and Loans and Debt Management.

His constituency activities have included education, youth empowerment, skills acquisition and community development initiatives. These efforts have included support for educational infrastructure and programmes designed to expand opportunities for young people.

In October 2024, he inaugurated a six-classroom block at St. Mary Primary School in Igbede, Ojo, while also supporting a STEM quiz and career initiative for girls in the constituency.

In December 2025, he distributed 20 tricycles to constituents as part of an empowerment initiative, highlighting youth empowerment and skills acquisition as important elements of his constituency agenda.

In December 2025, he also flagged off a skills acquisition centre in Era, Ojo, with programmes covering areas including digital skills, garment making, beauty and cosmetology, agro-processing, modern farming, mechanical and construction trades and financial literacy.

His political journey later moved from the Labour Party to the African Democratic Congress (ADC), before he joined the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) in May 2026 together with other members of the House of Representatives.

His current public-service message centres on representation, economic opportunity, human-capital development, education, skills acquisition and constituency development.
