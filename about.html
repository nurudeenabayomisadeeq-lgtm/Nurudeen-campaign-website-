Candidate Profile
🗳️ Lagos State House of Assembly

A Son of Ojo, Committed to Service

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is the IMOLE 2027 candidate for the Lagos State House of Assembly representing Ojo Constituency II under the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

His campaign is built around the principles of Service, Development and Accountability, with a focus on representing the interests and aspirations of the people of Ojo Constituency II.

Having grown up within the community and worked among its people, the campaign presents him as a candidate who understands the importance of accessibility, community engagement and responsible representation.

The IMOLE Vision

IMOLE 2027 is centered on a vision of responsive representation, community development and accountable public service. The campaign seeks to place the needs, aspirations and development of Ojo Constituency II at the centre of representation.

A Community-Centred Approach
