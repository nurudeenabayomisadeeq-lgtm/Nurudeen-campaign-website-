About the Candidate

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a dedicated public servant, community leader and passionate advocate for the people of Ojo Constituency II.

He is committed to transparent leadership, quality education, youth empowerment, improved healthcare, better roads, economic development and accountable governance.

His vision is to build a stronger, safer and more prosperous Ojo Constituency II where every citizen has the opportunity to succeed.

Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Accountability
  • Transparency
  • Service to the People
  • Youth Empowerment
  • Community Development
