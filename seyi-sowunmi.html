A public representative focused on bringing the concerns, aspirations and opportunities of Ojo Federal Constituency closer to effective representation.

Who He Is

Hon. Oluwaseyi Ayopo Sowunmi is a Nigerian politician and member of the House of Representatives representing Ojo Federal Constituency of Lagos State.


He entered the 10th House of Representatives as the elected representative of Ojo Federal Constituency following the 2023 general elections. Public parliamentary profiles identify him as a first-term member of the 10th House.

Representation With Purpose

For Ojo, representation should go beyond occupying a seat. It should mean listening to communities, supporting opportunities for young people and women, encouraging skills development, and ensuring that constituency concerns receive attention.


The IMOLE 2027 vision places emphasis on service, development, accountability and stronger community engagement.

Legislative Journey

Key milestones in Hon. Seyi Sowunmi's public legislative journey.

2023 — Elected to the House of Representatives

Hon. Seyi Sowunmi was elected to represent Ojo Federal Constituency in the 2023 general elections and became a member of the 10th House of Representatives.

House Committee Responsibilities

Parliamentary profiles identify him with committee responsibilities including Local Content and other legislative committees within the National Assembly.

Constituency Engagement

His constituency activities have included initiatives focused on education, STEM-related opportunities, skills development and empowerment.
