Published Manifesto

This page presents the published policy priorities and development proposals associated with the candidacy of Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq.

Infrastructure

Rehabilitation of roads, improved drainage systems and public infrastructure.

Education

Education support, school improvements and vocational training initiatives.

Healthcare

Improved access to healthcare services and stronger primary healthcare facilities.

Youth & Women

Skills development, entrepreneurship support and employment-related initiatives.

Accountability

Transparent and accountable representation and public-service responsibilities.

Constituency Focus

The proposals are presented in relation to issues affecting communities within Ojo Constituency II.
