Our Manifesto

Theme: Service • Development • Accountability

Our Commitments

  • Rehabilitation of roads and drainage systems.
  • Improved healthcare centres across Ojo Constituency II.
  • Support for quality education and school development.
  • Youth empowerment through skills acquisition and employment opportunities.
  • Women empowerment and support for small businesses.
  • Support for traders, artisans and local entrepreneurs.
  • Improved security through collaboration with relevant agencies.
  • Transparent, accountable and people-centred representation.
  • Regular constituency engagement and feedback meetings.
  • Promotion of peace, unity and sustainable community development.

Our Vision

To build a stronger, safer and more prosperous Ojo Constituency II where every resident has access to opportunities, quality public services and responsive representation.
