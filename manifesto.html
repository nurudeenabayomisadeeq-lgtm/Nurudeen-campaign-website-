Our Vision

Service That Reaches The People

The IMOLE 2027 vision is centered on practical representation, responsible leadership and development that responds to the everyday needs of residents of Ojo Constituency II.

💡

IMOLE — We Shine, We Serve

We believe representation should not end after an election. It should mean listening to communities, identifying their priorities, pursuing solutions and remaining accountable to the people.

Our Foundation

Three Pillars Of IMOLE 2027

Every part of the campaign vision is built around three guiding principles.

PILLAR 01
🤝

Service

Put the people first through accessible representation, community engagement, responsive leadership and practical advocacy on issues affecting Ojo Constituency II.

PILLAR 02
🏗️

PILLAR 03
⚖️

Promote responsible representation, transparency, regular engagement with constituents and clear communication about public responsibilities and constituency initiatives.

Development Agenda

Our Key Priorities

IMOLE 2027 will focus attention on areas that directly affect the quality of life and economic wellbeing of constituents.

🌧️

Drainage & Flooding

Advocate for attention to drainage challenges, flood-prone areas and environmental conditions that affect homes, businesses, roads and communities.

🛣️

Roads & Infrastructure

Champion improved roads, access routes, public infrastructure and maintenance of facilities that support movement, commerce and community development.

🏥

Healthcare

Advocate for stronger primary healthcare facilities, improved access to essential services and greater attention to the health needs of local communities.

🎓

Education

Support initiatives that improve learning environments, encourage educational development and create opportunities for young people to build productive futures.

🛍️

Traders & Small Businesses

Champion the interests of traders, artisans and small businesses by promoting an environment where local enterprise can grow and contribute to the constituency economy.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Youth, Women & Families

Encourage opportunities and programmes that support young people, women, families and community-based initiatives across Ojo Constituency II.

Responsible Representation

Leadership Must Be Accountable

IMOLE 2027 believes constituents deserve representation that listens, communicates and remains connected to the communities it serves.

  • Regular engagement with constituents and community stakeholders.
  • Listening to community priorities and raising them through appropriate legislative and governmental channels.
  • Clear communication about constituency activities and initiatives.
  • Respect for public resources and responsible leadership.
  • Continued accessibility to the people of Ojo Constituency II.

First 100 Days

A Practical Start To Representation

The first 100 days will focus on listening, assessment, engagement, advocacy and establishing clear priorities for constituency representation.

01

Constituency Listening Tour

Engage communities, traditional leaders, youth, women, traders and other stakeholders to identify urgent constituency priorities.

02

Infrastructure Assessment

Document key concerns around roads, drainage, public facilities and other infrastructure challenges requiring advocacy.

03

Healthcare & Education Review

Engage relevant stakeholders to understand pressing needs affecting healthcare and educational facilities in the constituency.

04

Youth & Women Engagement

Establish stronger channels for listening to youth and women and identifying practical opportunities for empowerment and participation.

05

Traders & Enterprise Engagement

Consult traders, artisans and small businesses about challenges affecting local commerce and identify areas for legislative advocacy.

06

Constituency Communication

Strengthen communication channels through which residents can receive updates and communicate constituency concerns.
