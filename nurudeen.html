Meet the Candidate

A Voice for Ojo Constituency II

Hon. Nurudeen Abayomi Sadeeq is a son of Ojo Constituency II and a passionate advocate for community development, responsible representation and accountable leadership.

His vision for Ojo is centred on practical development that directly improves the lives of residents — from better roads and drainage to stronger schools, accessible healthcare and meaningful opportunities for young people and traders.

The IMOLE 2027 movement is built around a simple principle: leadership must remain connected to the people it serves.

“Service. Development. Accountability.” A commitment to putting the interests, dignity and future of the people first.

Leadership Values

The IMOLE Standard

Leadership should be measured not only by promises, but by character, accessibility and results.

1

Integrity

Standing firmly for honesty and responsible public service.

2

Accessibility

Keeping leadership close to the people and responsive to their concerns.

3

Humility

Listening to communities and respecting every constituent.

4

Accountability

Being answerable to the people and transparent in public service.

5

Compassion

Putting people, families and communities at the heart of development.

Our Constituency

Listening to Ojo

The IMOLE movement believes that effective representation begins with listening.

🗣️ Your Voice Matters
